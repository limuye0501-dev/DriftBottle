Privacy Policy

Drift Bottle (“the app”, “we”) lets adults send anonymous messages in virtual bottles and chat with people who pick them up. This policy explains exactly what the app collects and why. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1. Who can use the app

The app is for people aged 18 and over. You must confirm your age when you start. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn an account belongs to a minor we delete it.

2. Data we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. You sign in anonymously through Firebase Authentication (no name, email, phone or password). This creates a random user ID.
  • Device identifier. The app's vendor identifier (identifierForVendor) is stored to enforce daily limits and bans per device, so they cannot be bypassed by reinstalling.
  • Profile. A nickname (optional, generated if left blank), your gender and age (stored as an approximate birth year). Gender and age are shown to people who read your bottles; they are not used to restrict who can see your bottles.
  • Your content. Bottle messages, photos you attach, replies and chat messages, and the letter style you choose.
  • Usage records. Counts of bottles thrown/picked, last-active time, and bottle status (picked, kept, thrown back), used to run the service and daily quotas.
  • Push token. A Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can notify you of replies (only if you allow notifications).
  • Language setting, to localise server messages.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; we receive and store the transaction/subscription status from Apple to unlock membership. If you explicitly opt in on the paywall, and Apple asks us about a refund request, we may share usage counts (never your messages) with Apple.
  • Reports and blocks. Users you block, and reports you submit or that are submitted about your content.

We do not collect your location, contacts, email, phone number, browsing history, advertising identifier, or health data. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. How we use it

Only to provide the app: deliver bottles to other users (any adult user may receive any bottle, excluding the sender, blocked users and removed content), enable chats, send notifications, enforce limits, unlock subscriptions, keep the community safe, and respond to support requests.

4. Moderation

Text is checked automatically against word and link filters. Uploaded photos are screened by Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch for explicit or violent content; blocked images are deleted. Reported content may be reviewed by us, and accounts/devices that break the rules may be banned. We do not use generative AI, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

5. Service providers

Data is stored and processed by Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging and Cloud Vision) acting as our processor, primarily in the European Union (europe-west1). Apple processes purchases and push delivery. We share data with no one else, except where required by law.

6. Visibility to other users

People who receive your bottle see its text, photo, your nickname, gender and age. People you chat with see your messages. Your account ID and device identifier are never shown.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, your bottles, your photos and your anonymous account. Chats you took part in are closed and your nickname is removed from them; messages already delivered to the other person may remain visible to them. To enforce bans and daily limits, a minimal device record (device identifier, quota counters, ban status) and moderation reports are retained. Deleting the app without deleting the account leaves your data in place; you can also ask us to delete it (see below).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to processing. Because accounts are anonymous, please include the nickname shown in the app's Settings when contacting us.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, open a request at github.com/limuye0501-dev/DriftBottle/issues. We respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date below.
