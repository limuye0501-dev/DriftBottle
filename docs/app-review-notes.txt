Guideline 2.1 — how to test Pick up a bottle and chat

Drift Bottle is anonymous and for adults 18+. No demo account is required. Sign-in happens automatically. A subscription is not required to pick up a bottle, reply, chat, report, or block.

On a brand-new install the public sea may not yet contain letters from other people. This build does not treat that as an empty sea.

1. Open the app. Confirm you are 18 or older and agree to the Terms of Use and Community Rules (the checkbox is required).
2. The Chats tab already has a welcome conversation with the Lighthouse Keeper. The Bottles tab already has a welcome letter. Neither list is empty on first launch.
3. On the Sea tab, tap Pick up a bottle. The first pick always opens a real letter you can read. When no other person's bottle is available, the letter is from the Lighthouse Keeper. The pick counts against today's picks.
4. Tap Write back, type a short reply, and send it. A chat opens, and the Lighthouse Keeper answers in that chat.
5. Throw it back returns the letter to the sea. Pick up a bottle again and you get a different letter (letters are not repeated until the set has all been read).
6. Throwing a bottle of your own does not make the sea look empty. Pick up a bottle still returns someone else's letter, or a Lighthouse Keeper letter if nobody else's is waiting. Letters in any language can be picked when none are available in the reviewer's language.
7. If the device is offline, Pick up a bottle shows a connection error and asks you to try again. It does not say the sea is quiet.

The same path works on iPhone and on iPad. Please use the Sea tab's Pick up a bottle button, not only the Bottles or Chats tabs.
