Support

Need help with Drift Bottle? Open a support request at github.com/limuye0501-dev/DriftBottle/issues. Please include the nickname shown in Settings and your device/iOS version.

FAQ

How do I report or block someone? Tap “…” on a bottle or chat and choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Drift Bottle. Use “Restore Purchases” on the membership screen after reinstalling.

How do I delete my account and data? Settings → Delete account. See the Privacy Policy.

Who can pick up my bottle? Any other adult user, except people you've blocked.
