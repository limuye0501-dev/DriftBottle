On the road for work and the hotel room is too quiet?
Write a few lines, seal them in a glass bottle and toss it into tonight's sea. Then pick up a bottle someone else set adrift and read a stranger's day.

• No sign-up: no phone number, no email — just open and write
• 3 free throws and 3 free picks every day; replies, chat and photos are always free
• Hand-crafted animations: bobbing bottles, a satisfying cork pop, a sky that follows the clock
• Pick a handwriting, Kai or typewriter font in three sizes; every bottle style and letter paper is free
• Moderation, reporting and blocking keep things calm

Not a social network — just a small pastime before sleep. 18+ only. Membership prices are shown in the App Store.
